Configure Districts
Auto-generate Builds a fresh set of districts. Min and max seats bound how large each district can be, and may be equal. Give a total number of deputies, or of districts, or both; whichever you leave blank is filled in at random. This replaces the list below.
Regions
Regions group districts so a party can poll differently in different parts of the country. On each region: + assigns every unassigned district to it, ✕ deletes the region but keeps its districts, 🗑 deletes the region and its districts.

Vote Transfers (STV)
Under STV voters rank candidates, so votes a party can't use aren't wasted: they move on. That happens when a party wins a seat with more votes than it needed (its surplus), and when a party is knocked out of the count (eliminated).
Each block below is one party. Kept is the share of its surplus that stays with the party to help elect its next candidate. Passed on to says where the rest goes, and where all its votes go if it is eliminated. None is the share of its voters who ranked nobody else; their ballots stop counting. The party rows and None add up to 100% (if they don't, the party rows are scaled to fit when you close). The starting values come from the parties' left-right positions.
