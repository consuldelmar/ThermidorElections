Configure Districts
Auto-generate Builds a fresh set of districts. Min and max seats bound how large each district can be, and may be equal. Give a total number of deputies, or of districts, or both; whichever you leave blank is filled in at random. This replaces the list below.
Regions
Regions group districts so a party can poll differently in different parts of the country. On each region: + assigns every unassigned district to it, ✕ deletes the region but keeps its districts, 🗑 deletes the region and its districts.

Vote Transfers (STV)
Under STV a voter ranks candidates rather than picking one, so votes a party cannot use are passed on rather than wasted. That happens twice: when a party clears the quota (the number of votes needed for a seat) with a surplus to spare, and when a trailing party is eliminated. Each block below is one party, and its sliders set which parties those transferred votes go to. The grey None slider is the share of voters who ranked nobody else, so those ballots exhaust and leave the count instead of transferring. The remaining weights are normalised to 100% when you close. They are auto-derived from left-right proximity and fully editable.
