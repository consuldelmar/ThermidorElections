Vote Transfers (STV)
When a party wins a seat with votes to spare, or is knocked out, its votes move on.
Kept: share of the spare votes the party keeps. Passed on to: where the rest goes (and everything, if knocked out). None: voters who ranked no one else. Votes always follow the row of the party they started with. Defaults come from the parties' positions; rows are scaled to 100% on close.
